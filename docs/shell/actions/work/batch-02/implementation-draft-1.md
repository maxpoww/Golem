# implementation-draft-1.md — batch 02

*Draft 1 of 3. P9 rewrites this from a blank page as `implementation-draft-2.md`,
then from a blank page again as the final `implementation.md`. Draft 1's job is to
get the batch's own material onto the table: every claim below should be traceable

---

**1.** ACTIONS began as a thousand habits and was expected to end near forty; the
second batch ends at twelve, and that narrowing is the argument against slop
rather than a taste claim. Fifteen entered this batch's first hunt and three were
killed on evidence read from the real tree: A07 because the shipped dock already
tucks itself away, A11 because no host virtualization exists on these machines,
A05 because the engine carries no cursor stream at all. What remains ships after a
second hunt over the finished blocks that repaired four rows and struck none.
Every survivor is deliberately small: a sensed condition, one sentence, one verb
in a nested pill, a right-click that costs nothing, and a palm-sized gear box that
grows out of the sentence that just spoke. The register spans nine categories in
twelve rows, and the count is stated plainly because the funnels cut by named
criteria and stopped where the razor found no padding: twelve is where usefulness
ran out, not where a quota was met.

**2.** The engine's discipline is that a detector is a pure function over streams
that already arrive — the audio collector's pw-mon watcher, the Hyprland event
socket, the zsh bridge's exit code, the downloads poll — and no detector owns a
timer. Two system laws bind every Fires line: the day's budget is a priority
queue ranked by expiry rather than by type, never two in an hour, no carryover,
expiring moments speaking and habit unlocks waiting; and one mouth per cluster
with back-of-queue on refusal, so A12 and A01 never share a moment. Acceptance is
a persistence question: evidence is counters, hashes, medians and histograms
under the launcher's stores, a signed rule one readable line in the rules
directory. The honest verdict: **two of the twelve can speak on
today's engine — A09 rides its fully-live shell bridge, desktop table and launch
seam, and A12 is a pure conjunction of two live booleans over a live `wpctl`
verb**. Nine wait on named cheap work in our own tree, one read or counter deeper
in a collector that already runs — the source-mute read, the pair-counters,
the reachable-test, the association memory, the console-class name, the eval-rail
write, and the one logind listener A10 and A14 share in dormancy. A13 waits on its
[new] EXIF actuator, the one seam that is not ours to ship.

**3.** The surface is inherited, not imagined: the twelve blocks take their
choreography from the sunset prototype that has been live since early September,
so the pill, the nested verbs, the gear and the grow morph are settled rather than
speculative. A12's gear shows the remembered level itself, visible and erasable,
because a stored preference the user cannot read is not a preference they own.
A02's gear spent one whole absorbed habit as a single control rather than
reciting it, and A06's gear is the entire learned map, one removable row per kind.
The one layout rule is carried from the earlier batch: the morph's width math
hardcodes a single inner label, so where the sentence plus pills would overflow, a
second verb is dropped, never truncated, and the offer must read the same on a
2013 Air as on a desk monitor. Signed rules live one readable line each under the
config rules directory; revocation is deleting the line, never a rebuild;
evidence lives beside the launcher's own stores.

**4.** Privacy is arithmetic, stated in each block as at-rest shape and
approximate bytes. Twelve lifecycle rows at about 32 bytes make the shared
baseline; A02 keeps a ring of sixteen hashed kind-destination pairs near 0.3 kB,
A08 a ring of eight near 0.2 kB, A10 a single 24-byte hour histogram, and A14 adds
nothing at all — its store already exists and is cleared, never created. A13 reads
a header transiently to know that metadata exists, never what it contains, and its
sentence is gated on that read so the offer never lies. Nothing nameable sits at
rest: a site, command, file or title is read live at the moment of speaking and
dropped, per name-at-offer-time. The register obeys one rule several of its rows
had to relearn: never learn from your own hand, so a launch performed by Golem
does not teach the launcher a ranking the user never made — the same lesson A17,
A38 and A39 taught the first batch.

**5.** Admission is not a gate passed once; the funnel is governance kept running
against the finished product. The first hunt killed three on engine evidence and
retired them without replacement, the second hunted the twelve finished blocks
field by field and repaired four rather than striking any, and every repair is
recorded where the fault sat rather than in the preamble. The ladder has real
shapes: A08 offers only its standing rule because the user's own repetitions were
the tutorial, A09 ships with no automatic rung because an auto-open of anything
matching would run apps nobody chose, and A12 never speaks for a user who never
accepted a level, by construction. A refusal is data that escalates toward
silence: two or three refusals retire an action, and an answered member of a
cluster steps to its back so the whole cluster drains and falls quiet. The
notebook closes the loop, every detector listed with its evidence, forgettable
per line.

**6.** The voice was never the hard part, and neither hunt killed on voice. The
twelve sentences pass the standing test — a fact about a thing is an observation,
a fact about a person is a verdict — because what remains is always evidence: a
muted source, a landed branch, a dead command, a store that holds data. A13's
sentence grew the discipline that protects it: because it claims the picture
still carries its metadata, the trigger now reads the header for the marker, so
the sentence is only offered when it is true. A01's sentence names a state the
user cannot feel, and that is why it is thankable rather than a nag. The feel that
follows is rarity: a dozen actions that mostly fall silent forever once accepted,
a download that moves once and rarely speaks again, a level set once and
remembered. Twelve sentences against a budget of three a day is a voice that
speaks least when the user needs it most.
