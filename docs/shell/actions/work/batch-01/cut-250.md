## Casualties worth noting

- The in-browser massacre (~25 kills: S0002–S0009, S0013–S0016, S0028–S0031, S0045–S0046, S0050–S0052…): tabs, bookmarks, reader mode, address-bar craft — a whole civilization of daily habits lives behind glass the engine cannot see through, because the browser bridge is BLOCKED under the guardrails (catalog §8). One architectural fact killed more habits than any judgment call tonight.
- S0007 reopen-closed-tab — the classic. The RECOVERY ships today as an OPTIONS control (browser.reopen_tab), but "the user just closed a tab" is invisible, so it can never be an ACTION that speaks first. The sharpest illustration of the OPTIONS/ACTIONS boundary in the whole list.
- The raw-keys discipline killed the shortcut habits (Ctrl+S S0228, Ctrl+Z S0229, Ctrl+F S0241/S0471, shell history keys S0202/S0203, select-tricks S0233): Constitution VI — keystrokes are out of scope, permanently. No exceptions were even tempting.
- S0264 wrong-layout-retype — the most relatable kill: detecting it requires reading what was typed. Died on the same law, honorably.
- Per-site title diaries (S0053 forum, S0130 sports, S0185 Stack Overflow, S0186 AI-app, S0217 ticket board, S0441 fun-loop, S0444 lunch errands, S0457 newsletter, S0458 puzzle, S0464 window-shopping): each is the same detector wearing a different site, and together they'd be a browsing diary. Kept exactly one morning-news carrier (T0002) and the two workflow-anchored ones (CI T0092, PR T0109); killed the rest as a family.
- S0462 parcel-tracking / S0463 portfolio — sensable via titles, but every offer worth making ("it moved!") requires the network the engine is structurally incapable of (Constitution VI.1). First candidates killed by the engine's own privacy architecture rather than by sensing.
- S0339/S0340 bluetooth reconnect-by-hand and earbud battery — need a bluez collector [new]; only the pipewire-visible half survives (T0167). S0350 the what-did-you-plug-in dialog died as hardware-niche.
- S0446/S0447 countdown-to-meeting and join-on-the-minute — calendar CONTENTS are a bridge the engine doesn't have; the mic-sensed standup sibling (T0020) carries the moment instead.
- The trait graveyard (window/workspace lost 37 of 51): maximizes-by-reflex (S0363–S0366), navigates-workspaces-blind (S0384), keyboard-only launching (S0274), sacred pinned order (S0281), taskbar preference (S0357) — a standing preference has no discrete recurring moment, hence no trigger, hence no sentence worth speaking. F3's real discovery: "sensable" must mean an EVENT, not a state of character.
- S0420 show-desktop-when-someone-approaches — the trigger is a human walking up; the OS senses only the flinch, always too late to help.
- S0218 timesheet reconstruction — the habit is remembering; an OS-built day-log to "help" would be the designer's imagination answering (Constitution III) and a diary besides (VI.3). Killed twice over.
- S0086/S0152/S0174 the drag-and-drop family — Wayland drags happen entirely inside the apps; the compositor never hears about them.
- S0330 power-strip-off — dies at the wall socket, one inch past the OS's last sensable moment.
- S0406 locks-at-office-not-home — needs location, which this engine will never have; killed without appeal.
