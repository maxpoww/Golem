## Casualties worth noting

- H0059 (browsing) tab bankruptcy — the purge is episodic; the daily habit is the hoarding, which survives as S0005/S0006.
- H0228 (files) camera-card photo import — the constitution's own §III example of a specific moment, killed honestly: not roughly daily even for hobbyists; a natural returnee for the weekly tier (batch ≥3).
- H0727 (security) webcam cover ([observed] HP 2019, ~60%) — real and daily, but sliding a piece of plastic leaves no footprint a desktop OS could ever see.
- H0712 (security) passwords in a paper notebook — the purest F2 kill in the list: common, daily-adjacent, zero digital footprint by design.
- H0704 (security) password reuse ([observed] GoDaddy 2025, 61%) — a fact about people, not a daily act; nothing happens on any given day to sense.
- H0803 (health) break reminder obeyed twice, then dismissed forever — episodic, but design gold: it documents exactly how reminder-apps die, which is what ACTIONS must never become.
- health/ergonomics lost 59 of 65 — stretching, posture, water, and eye rituals are invisible to a desktop OS; only the screen-side acts survive (evening font bump, brightness-torch moment, listening-fatigue volume dip, earbuds-to-speakers switch, lid-close pause).
- finance/shopping lost 56 of 60 — checkouts, coupons, bills, and comparisons are per-purchase, not per-day; only the daily glances survive (bank balance, portfolio, parcel-tracking streaks, window-shopping).
- H0891/H0892 (finance) cart abandonment ([observed] Baymard, ~70%) — the best-documented behavior in the entire 1000, and still per-purchase; data quality cannot overrule the daily bar.
- H0410 + H0524 reboot-as-universal-fix — iconic, but reactive; the whole when-it-breaks family died with it (wifi toggle H0395, task-manager kill H0400, audio-stack restart H0571, monitor replug H0606): troubleshooting is a genre of its own, not a daily action.
- H0715 (security) approving the 2FA push without reading it — phone-only; the desktop sees only the login flow around it (which survives as S0414/H0713).
- H0929 (finance) splitting the dinner bill in the payment app — phone-only; the desktop never meets this moment.
- travel family (H0431 timezone fix, H0525 flight shutdown, H0554 charge-everything-the-night-before, H0537 charger left at home) — computing's most cinematic rituals are, by definition, not everyday.
- H0866–H0869 (time-of-day) Friday cleanup, Saturday off, Sunday preview, Monday-plan-on-Sunday — real and loved, and weekly by definition; earmarked for the weekly tier.
- H0864 (time-of-day) "one more episode" — the same moment as autoplay-past-the-planned-stop (H0145/S0118), which survives; killed as a duplicate, not as untrue.
- H0517 (power) brightness-down-as-the-room-darkens — killed as the generic twin of H0809/S0430 (display turns torch when the room goes dark), keeping the sharper, sunset-adjacent form.
