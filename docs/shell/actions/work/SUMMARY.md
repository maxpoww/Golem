## The two ratified laws (binding on every later batch)

1. **The day's budget is a PRIORITY QUEUE ranked by EXPIRY, not by type.** A16 is
   the proof — a pending update is a moment whose value does not die with the
   moment. Expiring moments outrank habit unlocks and spend the allowance; unlocks
   wait indefinitely; never two unlocks in an hour; no carryover; no detector owns
   a timer.
2. **One mouth per cluster, back-of-the-queue on refusal.** Without it, "a refusal
   is data" becomes "a refusal is ignored". A refused offer rejoins the back of its
   cluster's queue; a second "not now" on the same offer silences it until the user
   applies an update by hand.
